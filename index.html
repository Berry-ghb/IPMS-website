Why IT-Connect exists

The Problem With Placement Search Today

Industrial Training (IT) — known formally as SIWES, the Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme — is mandatory for most Nigerian university, polytechnic, and college-of-education students, coordinated by the Industrial Training Fund (ITF). Despite being compulsory, finding a placement is left almost entirely to the individual student.

Students commonly go office to office submitting printed application letters and repeating the trip when there's no reply — a process well documented as a source of frustration in Nigerian SIWES research, and one that costs money in transport and printing before any training even begins.

Manual, Locational Search

Students go from office to office with printed letters, repeating the trip if there's no reply, with no guarantee of a response.

Costs Before Training Begins

Transport fares, printing, and lost time are absorbed entirely by students — many of whom have no income yet.

Information Asymmetry

Students rarely know in advance which organisations are accepting trainees, how many slots exist, or what skills are required — so effort is wasted on dead ends.

Course-Mismatch Placements

Because finding any placement is hard, students often end up in organisations unrelated to their field of study, undermining the purpose of IT.

Fraud Risk

Unregistered "organisations" have been reported requesting payment for guaranteed placement, exploiting the pressure students are under.

No Visibility for Schools

SIWES coordinators struggle to track, verify, and supervise students once they scatter across informally-found placements.

The Core Workflow

Capacity-Aware Postings, One-Profile Applications

This single change — organisations stating exactly how many slots they have, and students applying once with a saved profile — is what removes the physical, door-to-door search entirely.

1

Organisation registers and gets verified

Then posts an opening with a defined number of available slots, required course(s) of study, duration, and location.

2

Student builds one profile

Institution, course, level, location preference, skills, and CV — built once, reused for every application.

3

Student searches or gets matched

Filters by course, location, and duration — or receives suggested postings based on their profile — then applies in one click. No printing, no physical delivery.

4

Organisation reviews applicants

A dashboard lets them filter by course/skills and shortlist or message candidates directly in-platform.

5

Slots fill and close automatically

Once the organisation accepts students up to its stated capacity, the posting closes — so no one wastes time applying to a full slot.

6

Both sides get a confirmation record

The student gets proof of placement for their school; the organisation gets a record of who is arriving and when.

Built on Trust & Safety

Designed Against the Exact Risks Students Already Face

Marketplace research is consistent on one point: platforms fail not from missing features, but from users not trusting the other side enough to transact. For IT-Connect, the stakes are concrete.

Strict No-Payment Policy

We never allow or facilitate any request for payment from a student in exchange for placement. Organisations must provide CAC registration or an institutional ID before their first posting goes live.

Verified organisations only

CAC registration, an official company email domain (not a free Gmail/Yahoo address), and a physical address that can be cross-checked.

Conversations stay in-platform

Early messaging happens on IT-Connect rather than being pushed straight to WhatsApp or phone, so there's a record if something goes wrong.

Verified reviews

Students and organisations can review each other after placement — only users with a confirmed placement can leave one.

Report & moderate

A simple report/flag button on every posting and message thread, routed straight to an admin moderation queue.

Transparent dispute handling

Clear rules for what happens if an organisation doesn't honour an accepted slot, or a student ghosts after acceptance.

Platform-wide oversight

Admins track active postings, fill rate, average time-to-placement, and geographic demand to catch issues early.
