Nigeria's Official SIWES Marketplace

Bridging the gap between classroom and industry one placement at a time.

IPMS (IT-Connect) is a two-sided platform designed to close the long-standing gap between students who need Industrial Training (IT/SIWES) placements and the organisations willing to host them[cite: 1].

End the Manual Placement Search

We are replacing door-to-door, transport-fare-heavy placement hunting with a structured, transparent, online process[cite: 1].

Zero Transport Costs

Stop spending money going from office to office dropping off printed CVs. Apply to multiple verified placements from your phone[cite: 1].

Eradicate Fraud

Unregistered "organisations" requesting payment for guaranteed placement exploit students. Our strict verification prevents placement fraud[cite: 1].

Perfect Course Matching

Stop settling for placements unrelated to your field of study. Organizations specify exactly what courses they need, ensuring relevant training[cite: 1].

A Unified Ecosystem

Whether you are seeking training, hiring trainees, or managing a school department, IPMS connects you seamlessly[cite: 1].

For Students

Build a single profile and apply to as many postings as fit your course and location[cite: 1]. Track your application status, message employers directly, and download placement confirmation letters[cite: 1].

For Organisations

Post available IT/SIWES slots stating role, number of openings, required course of study, and duration[cite: 1]. Review applicants, shortlist candidates, and fill slots efficiently[cite: 1].

For Coordinators

A lightweight view that lets a department confirm which of its students have secured verified placements, drastically reducing paperwork[cite: 1].

Built on Trust & Safety

We verify every organization to ensure a safe environment for students[cite: 1].

Strict No-Payment Policy

We never allow or facilitate any request for payment from a student in exchange for placement[cite: 1]. Organizations must provide CAC registration or institutional IDs to post slots[cite: 1].
